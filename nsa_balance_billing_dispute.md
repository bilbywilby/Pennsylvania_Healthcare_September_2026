# No Surprises Act (NSA) Dispute Letter

**Purpose:** Cease collection activity and void balance bills issued by
out-of-network providers operating at in-network facilities.

---

To: [Provider Billing Department]
CC: [Insurance Carrier Claims / Appeals Department]
Date: [Date]
Subject: Dispute of Balance Bill – No Surprises Act Violation

**Notice:** Receipt of statement requesting payment of $[Amount] for services
rendered on [Date] at facility [Facility Name].

**Legal Basis:** Under the federal No Surprises Act (H.R. 133), patient
cost-sharing for emergency services, or non-emergency services delivered by
out-of-network providers at in-network facilities, is limited strictly to the
in-network Qualifying Payment Amount (QPA) determined by the insurance
carrier.

**Request:** Cease all collection activity, late-fee assessments, and credit
bureau reporting on this account immediately. Issue an updated billing
statement reflecting solely the in-network cost-sharing obligations
established on the Explanation of Benefits (EOB) issued by the insurance
carrier.

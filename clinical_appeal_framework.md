# Standard Clinical Appeal Framework

**Purpose:** Formally contest adverse benefit determinations citing medical
necessity exclusions.

---

To: [Insurance Carrier Appeals Department]
From: [Your Name]
Policy ID: [ID Number] | Claim #: [Claim Number]
Date of Service: [Date]
Subject: Formal Appeal of Denial for [Procedure/Service]

**Reference to Evidence of Coverage (EOC):** Pursuant to Section [Insert
Section Number from EOC] of the Evidence of Coverage, this service is defined
as a covered benefit when contractual clinical criteria are satisfied.

**Clinical Justification:** The treating provider has determined that this
service is medically necessary based on diagnosis [Specific Diagnosis] and
diagnostic history.

**Request for Review:** Request a full clinical review conducted by a
physician board-certified in [Medical Field]. If the denial is sustained,
demand the specific clinical peer-review guidelines utilized and the
credentials of the reviewing medical officer.

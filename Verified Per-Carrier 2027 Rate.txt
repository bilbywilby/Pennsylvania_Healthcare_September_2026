### 9.1 Filing Timeline and Context

Insurers participating in Pennsylvania's individual and small group markets submitted proposed 2027 rates to the Pennsylvania Insurance Department (PID) by the May 15, 2026 filing deadline. PID published the received filings on July 23, 2026, opening a formal public comment period through August 22, 2026 (PID, 2026). The requested rates arrive against the demand-side backdrop documented in prior sections: the expiration of enhanced premium tax credits in December 2025 roughly doubled net monthly premiums for marketplace enrollees, precipitating the cancellation series documented in Section 8. The 2027 filings represent the first pricing cycle in which carriers priced that observed attrition into their risk pools.

### 9.2 Individual Market Requests

Filings for the individual marketplace reflect an enrollment-weighted average requested increase of 17.1% (PID, 2026). Per-carrier requests range from Geisinger Health Plan at 10.46% (Rating Areas 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 9) to Ambetter Health of Pennsylvania at 40.90% across all rating areas (PID, 2026). PID's release explicitly decomposes Ambetter's request into a 23.44% silver-to-silver base increase plus a 17.46% bronze-to-silver mapping differential driven by Ambetter’s planned exit from the bronze metal tier for Plan Year 2027 (PID, 2026). Discontinued bronze enrollees retain access to bronze plans through alternative participating carriers. Additional regional carriers filed notable increases, including Keystone Health Plan Central at 33.83% and Highmark Benefits Group at 21.40% in Rating Areas 3 and 8 (PID, 2026).

### 9.3 Small Group Market Requests

Small group filings reflect an enrollment-weighted average requested increase of 11.5% (PID, 2026). Requested changes range from Geisinger Quality Options at 4.05% to Highmark Care Benefits at 19.95% (PID, 2026). Highmark Coverage Advantage submitted a 14.63% requested increase across Rating Areas 1, 2, 4, 5, and 6, while UnitedHealthcare’s filing incorporated plan structural mappings transitioning Gold and Silver small-group offerings into Bronze tier structures (PID, 2026).

### 9.4 Updated Attrition Series

Pennie's affordability dashboard (updated August 10, 2026) supersedes the July 1 figures reported in prior drafts. As of August 1, 2026, active enrollment stood at 422,869, reflecting 108,000 consumers dropping coverage since the close of OE 2026 on January 31, and over 191,000 cumulative cancellations when including open enrollment terminations (Pennie, 2026). The verified series—486,000 (Feb 1), 471,442 (March 24), 452,000 (May 1), 431,270 (July 1), and 422,869 (Aug 1)—maintains the standard reporting separation between open enrollment non-effectuations (85,000) and post-OE mid-year drops.

### 9.5 Regulatory Volatility: OE 2027 Schedule Reversal

The open enrollment schedule for Plan Year 2027 changed twice within five months. In March 2026, Pennie's board voted to shorten OE 2027 to October 15–December 15, conforming to federal regulations capping exchange open enrollment windows at nine weeks (PORH, 2026a). On July 16, 2026, the U.S. District Court for the District of Maryland stayed the federal duration restrictions in *City of Columbus v. Kennedy (Columbus II)*, No. 26-2215 (D. Md.) (CMS, 2026). Following subsequent CMS administrative guidance issued July 31, Pennie reinstated the traditional fifteen-week window: Open Enrollment 2027 will run **November 1, 2026 through January 15, 2027**, with coverage effective January 1 or February 1 depending on selection date (PHLP, 2026).

This reversal restores coverage access past the January 1 plan threshold for non-effectuated enrollees. However, the litigation-driven shift highlights that marketplace rules are subject to ongoing judicial challenge. Final 2027 rates remain subject to PID review; PID's official release states final approved rates will be published in the fall (PID, 2026).

## Executive summary

Pennsylvania insurers requested an enrollment-weighted average premium increase of **17.1% in the individual market** and **11.5% in the small-group market** for Plan Year 2027. The Pennsylvania Insurance Department stated that final rates would be published in the fall after review of the filings, objections, and public comments (Pennsylvania Insurance Department [PID], 2026). <citation src="2"></citation>

The largest individual-market request was **Ambetter Health of Pennsylvania at 40.90%**. PID explained that this figure combines a **23.44% silver-to-silver increase** with a **17.46% bronze-to-silver mapping differential** resulting from Ambetter’s planned withdrawal from the bronze tier. Consumers losing Ambetter bronze plans were expected to have access to bronze plans offered by other carriers (PID, 2026). <citation src="2"></citation>

The 2027 open-enrollment schedule also changed. Pennie initially moved toward a shorter October 15–December 15 enrollment period, but litigation over the federal nine-week limit resulted in restoration of the traditional **November 1, 2026–January 15, 2027** schedule (Pennsylvania Office of Rural Health [PORH], 2026). <citation src="5"></citation>

From February 1 to August 1, the reported active enrollment declined by **63,131 people**, or approximately **13.0%**. The supplied August figures distinguish between:

- **85,000 open-enrollment non-effectuations**, meaning people who selected coverage but did not complete the steps necessary for coverage to take effect; and
- **More than 191,000 cumulative cancellations** when open-enrollment terminations are included.

Those categories should not be added together without confirming whether they are mutually exclusive. The August 10 dashboard also reported that 108,000 consumers had dropped coverage since the close of Open Enrollment 2026, while the cumulative figure included open-enrollment terminations.

The reported attrition occurred after enhanced federal premium tax credits expired at the end of 2025. ## Open Enrollment 2027 schedule

The revised schedule is:

- **Open Enrollment begins:** November 1, 2026
- **Open Enrollment ends:** January 15, 2027
- **Coverage effective January 1:** For selections made by the applicable December deadline
- **Coverage effective February 1:** For selections made later during the enrollment period

The schedule was restored after the U.S. District Court for the District of Maryland stayed provisions of the 2027 Notice of Benefit and Payment Parameters that would have limited exchange open enrollment to nine weeks. Pennie subsequently reinstated the November 1–January 15 schedule (PORH, 2026). <citation src="5"></citation>

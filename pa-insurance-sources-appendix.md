# Appendix: Verified Data Sources — Pennsylvania Health Insurance Market

*Verified live as of September 2026. URLs corrected where sites have restructured.*

## Primary Regulatory & Marketplace Sources

**Pennsylvania Insurance Department.** (2026). *Affordable Care Act health rate filings, plan year 2027*. Commonwealth of Pennsylvania. https://www.pa.gov/agencies/insurance/posted-filings-reports-company-orders/posted-product-and-rate-filings/aca-health-rate-filings

- Verified live. Contains full carrier-by-carrier requested rate change %, 2026 market share, and links to per-carrier rate change summary PDFs, individual and small-group markets separately.
- Note: legacy URL `insurance.pa.gov` now redirects here; the site's navigation structure changed from what may be described in older notes ("Market Conduct & Compliance" no longer exists as a section — use "Filing and Reporting Requirements" and "Posted Filings, Reports and Orders" instead).
- Archived prior years: https://www.pa.gov/agencies/insurance/posted-filings-reports-company-orders/posted-product-and-rate-filings/aca-health-rate-filings/archived-health-insurance-rate-filings

**Pennie (Pennsylvania Health Insurance Exchange Authority).** (2026). *What's new for 2026*. https://pennie.com/costs/

- Verified live. Covers EPTC expiration (Dec. 31, 2025), premium impact, and eligibility changes under H.R. 1 (2025).
- Deeper data: Pennie's Affordability page (linked from the above) and https://agency.pennie.com/newsroom/ for press releases.

**Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services.** (2026). *Marketplace 2026 open enrollment period report: National snapshot*. U.S. Department of Health and Human Services. https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheets/marketplace-2026-open-enrollment-period-report-national-snapshot

- Verified live. State-by-state plan selection counts; confirms PA operates as a state-based exchange (SBE) for 2026, not a federally-facilitated marketplace.

## Supplementary Sources

**Pennsylvania Health Care Cost Containment Council.** (2026). *Reports library*. https://www.phc4.org/reports-library/

- Verified live. Scope caveat: PHC4's output is hospital-level cost/quality/utilization data (cardiac procedures, cancer surgery volumes, hospital financial analysis), not insurer premium or rate data. Useful for provider-cost context, not a substitute for PID rate filings.

**Kaiser Family Foundation.** (2026). *Marketplace average monthly benchmark premiums*. State Health Facts. https://www.kff.org/affordable-care-act/state-indicator/marketplace-average-benchmark-premiums/

- Verified live with 2026 data. Cross-check any marketplace-type classification for PA against CMS's SBE list above — one KFF indicator table returned a possibly stale "Federally Facilitated Marketplace" label for PA that conflicts with its actual SBE status.

**Federal Trade Commission, Bureau of Competition.** https://www.ftc.gov

- Not independently re-verified this pass. Use for antitrust/merger context (e.g., Highmark/UPMC) rather than rate data.

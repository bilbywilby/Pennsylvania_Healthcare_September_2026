# Pharmacy Tier / Formulary Exception Request

**Purpose:** Request a lower cost-sharing tier or coverage authorization for a
non-formulary pharmaceutical.

---

To: [Pharmacy Benefit Manager (PBM) Name]
Subject: Request for Formulary Exception / Tier Exception
Patient Name: [Name] | Prescription: [Drug Name]

**Clinical Grounds for Exception** (check applicable):

- [ ] **Contraindication:** Documented allergy or severe adverse clinical
  reaction to formulary-preferred alternatives: [Alternative Drugs].
- [ ] **Clinical Superiority / Step Therapy Failure:** The requested
  medication represents the medical standard of care for [Condition], and
  preferred formulary alternatives have been trialed without therapeutic
  efficacy.
- [ ] **Chronic Maintenance Stability:** The patient is clinically stabilized
  on this medication; substitution introduces substantial risk of adverse
  outcomes or acute disease relapse.

**Mandatory Requirement:** Provide the official Prior Authorization or
Formulary Exception Criteria Form required for the prescribing physician to
submit supporting clinical records.

purposes only and does not constitute legal or medical advice.*

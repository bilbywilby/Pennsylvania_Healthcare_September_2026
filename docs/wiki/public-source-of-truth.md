# Public Source Of Truth

## Purpose

This wiki explains Pennsylvania health insurance evidence for residents,
journalists, advocates, and reviewers. It is a research and public education
resource, not an insurance application, legal opinion, or substitute for a
licensed assister.

The project records three different kinds of information:

- **Observed or approved:** a published administrative result or a dataset
  that has passed the repository's validation gates.
- **Proposed or reported:** a filing, projection, or secondary report that may
  change during review.
- **Unresolved:** a claim that still needs a primary source or schema check.

## Current Scope

The repository covers the 2026 Pennsylvania individual and small-group ACA
markets, Pennie enrollment changes, the nine-county rating-area crosswalk, and
proposed Plan Year 2027 filings where clearly marked. Carrier percentages are
market-level evidence; they are not a personal premium quote.

The data boundary is important: the current public filing dataset is
carrier-level. It does not contain every household's age, income, plan, tax
credit, or final monthly premium. See [data and provenance](data-and-provenance.md).

## How To Read A Number

Check the label before comparing figures. An approved rate change, a requested
rate filing, an enrollment count, and a calculated estimate answer different
questions. A statewide weighted average also cannot be reproduced by taking a
simple average of carrier percentages.

## Maintenance Promise

Every material page should name its source, date, status, and next verification
step. Corrections should explain what changed. The repository's source
reliability appendix records exclusions and unresolved claims rather than
silently deleting inconvenient evidence.

For immediate coverage or eligibility decisions, confirm current information
with [Pennie](https://pennie.com/), the Pennsylvania Insurance Department, or
an authorized assister.

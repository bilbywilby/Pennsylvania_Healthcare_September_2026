# Data And Provenance

The current audit dataset is carrier-level filing data, not a consumer quote
table. `data/public/approved_rates_2026.csv` is therefore validated as a
carrier filing artifact with approved rate changes and rating areas.

The county crosswalk is generated by `02_crosswalk.py` and written to
`data/public/pa_county_fips_crosswalk.json`. A future consumer-level extract
must use a distinct filename and document its own source, schema, and review
status before entering the pipeline.

For unresolved source questions, use `source_reliability.md` and preserve the
distinction between verified facts, modeled values, and open follow-up work.

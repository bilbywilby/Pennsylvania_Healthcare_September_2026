# Pennie In 2026

## What Pennie Is

[Pennie](https://pennie.com/) is Pennsylvania's official health insurance
marketplace. It is where eligible residents compare ACA marketplace plans,
apply for financial help, and enroll in coverage. Plan availability, premiums,
and financial assistance depend on the household and the plan year; the audit
cannot determine an individual's eligibility.

## What This Project Records

The project records a decline in active Pennie enrollment from 486,000 on
February 1, 2026, to 422,869 on August 1, 2026, a reported decrease of 63,131
people, or approximately 13.0%. These are supplied administrative figures,
not a prediction of any particular resident's coverage status. The repository
also distinguishes open-enrollment non-effectuations from later cancellations
where the source data makes that distinction.

The project links the enrollment discussion to the reported expiration of
enhanced federal premium tax credits after December 31, 2025. Readers should
review current federal and Pennie guidance before assuming how tax credits
apply to their household.

## Enrollment Timing

The repository's September 2026 research records a restored 15-week window
for Plan Year 2027, described as November 1, 2026 through January 15, 2027,
after litigation over a proposed shorter schedule. This is a time-sensitive
record. Confirm the live deadline and any special enrollment options directly
with [Pennie's enrollment information](https://pennie.com/learn/).

## Practical Checklist

1. Update household, income, and coverage information in Pennie before
   comparing plans.
2. Compare total yearly cost, deductible, out-of-pocket maximum, provider
   network, prescriptions, and not only the monthly premium.
3. Save eligibility notices, plan selections, payment confirmations, and
   insurer correspondence.
4. Use Pennie's official help channels or a certified assister when a notice,
   deadline, or plan transition is unclear.

# Patient Advocacy Guide

This is an information checklist, not legal advice. Deadlines and appeal
rights vary by plan, employer, insurer, and issue.

## When A Bill Or Notice Looks Wrong

Keep the explanation of benefits, bill, denial notice, medical records that
support the request, and every insurer message together. Write down the date,
the person contacted, the reference number, and the requested resolution. Ask
the insurer to explain the denial, network status, cost-sharing calculation,
and appeal deadline in writing.

## Coverage And Medical Appeals

Follow the appeal instructions on the denial notice. Ask whether an expedited
review is available when waiting could seriously harm health. A clinician can
help explain medical necessity, prior treatment, and why the requested care
fits the plan's coverage terms. Do not send more medical information than the
review requires.

Pennsylvania's independent external review process and federal protections can
depend on plan type and denial type. Start with the official instructions from
the insurer and the [Pennsylvania Insurance
Department](https://www.pa.gov/agencies/insurance), then seek a certified
consumer assister or qualified legal aid when the issue is complex.

## Surprise Or Balance Billing

Ask whether the No Surprises Act applies before paying an unexpected
out-of-network emergency or facility bill. Keep the good-faith estimate,
consent forms, claim explanation, and bill. Federal guidance is available from
[CMS](https://www.cms.gov/nosurprises); applicability depends on the service,
facility, and coverage.

## Public Accountability

For a market-wide question, use the source links and public records process
rather than publishing another person's protected information. The project's
[RTKL materials](../../PA%20RTKL%20Request%20Operational%20Guide.md) can help
organize a records request. A records request is not an appeal and does not
pause an insurance deadline.

## Privacy Reminder

Redact member IDs, Social Security numbers, dates of birth, addresses, and
clinical details before sharing documents for review. This repository is not a
place to store a patient's case file.

# Section 9: 2027 Rate Filing Analysis & Continued Marketplace Attrition

## 9.1 Filing Timeline and Request Context

On May 15, 2026, health insurance carriers operating in Pennsylvania submitted their proposed rate filings for plan year 2027 to the Pennsylvania Insurance Department (PID). On July 23, 2026, PID formally published these proposed rates, initiating a public comment window that ran through August 22, 2026 (via `ra-in-comment@pa.gov`). 

These rate requests represent the first full filing cycle constructed entirely under the post-expiration baseline following the sunset of enhanced American Rescue Plan Act (ARPA) and Inflation Reduction Act (IRA) premium tax credits. The cost pressures documented during the 2026 mid-year attrition phase—driven by elevated risk-pool morbidity as lower-income, healthier enrollees exited—have now directly materialized in carrier pricing models for 2027.

Consistent with regulatory standards, all figures published in July 2026 reflect **requested** rate adjustments. These submissions remain subject to PID’s actuarial review and objection process before final approved rates are issued in late autumn.

## 9.2 Individual Market Rate Requests

For the individual market, 13 marketplace insurers submitted filings yielding an overall **enrollment-weighted average requested increase of 17.1%**. Requested adjustments across carriers range from a low of 9.68% to a high of 40.9%.

| Insurer / Entity | Requested Weighted Change (%) | Actuarial Composition & Key Drivers |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Ambetter (PA Health & Wellness)** | $+40.90\%$ | Consists of a $+23.44\%$ Silver-to-Silver rate increase combined with a $+17.46\%$ shift resulting from the complete discontinuation of Bronze plan offerings. |
| **Highmark Inc. / Benefits Group** | $+18.50\%$ | Adjusted for morbidity shifts following post-subsidy lapse; incorporates consolidation of prior Highmark Coverage Advantage enrollees. |
| **UPMC Health Options** | $+15.20\%$ | Medical cost trend and utilization adjustments in urban rating areas. |
| **Geisinger Health Plan (HMO)** | $+9.68\%$ | Lowest requested average increase in the individual market, reflecting regional risk stabilization. |
| **Market-Wide Weighted Average** | **$+17.10\%$** | **Aggregate weighted average across all 13 individual market filings.** |

### Key Structural Disaggregations

1. **Ambetter Plan Discontinuation vs. Rate Hike:** Reporting Ambetter’s requested $40.9\%$ adjustment purely as a rate hike misrepresents the filing mechanics. According to PID disclosures, the request decomposes into a **$23.44\%$ Silver-to-Silver baseline increase** and a **$17.46\%$ impact caused by Ambetter exiting the Bronze metal tier entirely**. Actuarial rules require automatic cross-walk reallocations into Silver tiers, driving up the effective premium cost. Affected enrollees maintain access to Bronze tier products offered by competing marketplace carriers within their rating areas.
2. **Carrier Horizon Consolidation:** Highmark Coverage Advantage submitted no independent rate filing for 2027. Existing enrollees are scheduled to consolidate into Highmark Inc. / Highmark Benefits Group entities, representing a minor structural consolidation within the marketplace.

## 9.3 Small Group Market Requests

Filings for the small group market demonstrated greater relative stability, with an aggregate **enrollment-weighted average requested increase of 11.5%**. 

Variance across small group carriers remains pronounced:
* **Geisinger Health Plan:** Submitted requests at the lower bound of the market, ranging between $+3.7\%$ and $+4.1\%$.
* **Highmark (Lehigh Valley Flex Blue PPO):** Submitted the highest small group request at $+21.4\%$, citing localized provider network utilization costs and small-employer group risk migration.

## 9.4 Post-Subsidy Enrollment Attrition Progression

Updated administrative data from Pennie (as of August 1, 2026) confirms continued steady attrition following the subsidy expiration. Total active enrollment fell to **422,869**, down from a peak of 486,000 at the close of Open Enrollment on February 1, 2026—representing a cumulative **13.0% decline ($63,131$ enrollees)**.

## 9.5 OE 2027 Schedule Realignment and Regulatory Outlook

### Open Enrollment Schedule Realignment
While secondary media accounts previously cited a traditional November 1 start date, administrative releases from the Pennsylvania Health Insurance Exchange Authority (PHIEA) confirm that **Open Enrollment 2027 will run from October 15, 2026, through December 15, 2026**. 

Under this updated federal and state schedule, **December 15 serves as the single final deadline** for coverage effective January 1, 2027. This 60-day window compresses the decision timeline for consumers compared to prior multi-stage deadlines, intensifying operational demands on marketplace navigators and enrollment assisters.

Final approved rates from Insurance Commissioner Michael Humphreys are anticipated in early autumn 2026.
